The answers at a glance

Question 1

Do full-time faculty stay within the Ministry cap?

The Ministry sets 24 credits per academic year for FT faculty. This can be raised to 30, and it has temporarily been allowed up to 36, provided the extra 12 credits are sections of courses the faculty member already teaches.

Question 3

Extra-course rule: who complies and who exceeds?

FT faculty may teach 2 extra courses per semester beyond their contract load, or 3 if they have an administrative release. The check is made separately for Fall and for Spring. Summer is not part of this rule.

    Method and assumptions

    Scenarios
    "Without summer" = Fall + Spring credits. "With summer" = Fall + Spring + Summer credits.
    Ministry bands (FT)
    Up to 24 = within the base cap · over 24 to 30 = within the extended cap · over 30 to 36 = within the temporary cap · over 36 = above every cap.
    Contract load
    The file's "Final Load" (Yearly Load minus Course release). Contract per semester = Final Load ÷ 2.
    Extra-course rule
    A semester complies if its credits are no more than (contract ÷ 2) + 2 courses × 3 credits, or + 3 courses × 3 credits when the faculty member has a release (Course release > 0). Fall and Spring are checked separately.
    Credits per course
    Assumed to be 3. Many loads in the file are fractional (e.g. 22.5, 35.25), probably because of lab weighting, so "courses" here means credit-equivalents.
    Extra FT positions needed
    Credits above the cap ÷ 24: the number of additional FT faculty at the base load needed to absorb the excess.
    Not verifiable from this file
    Whether the extra 12 credits under the temporary 36-credit cap are sections of courses already taught (the file has no course-level data).
